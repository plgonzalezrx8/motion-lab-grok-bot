# Earbuds A/B: vague prompt vs directed prompt (3D product shot)

Same subject, same technical line (one HTML file, three.js r160, 960x1080 per side, 6 s loop). Only the direction changes.

- `prompt-A-vague.txt`: 27 words ("cool, premium, modern"). Expect the default look: dark navy, glow ring, bloom, particles, spinning product, a made-up tagline.
- `prompt-B-directed.txt`: 448 words. GOAL/OUTPUT/TIME/MODEL/LOOK/SHOTS/CAMERA/BANNED/QA filled in with materials, three-point lighting, anticipation, springs and a ban list.
- `followups.txt`: 15 follow-ups in 4 review rounds (stills, pixel measurements, isolated glints, a frame-perfect pass).
- `sound.txt`: the score prompt and 12 sound follow-ups in 4 rounds, all from measurements (energy under 60 Hz, phone-band loudness, hit-over-bed levels, peak timing, AAC true-peak overshoot).

What changed between A and B: "premium" became materials, "cool" became a lit studio, "modern" became a camera, "animation" became beats and springs, and the defaults got banned by name. Every adjective you replace with a number is a decision you take back.

Original receipts: A about 30 min, untouched; B about 90 min with 15 follow-ups, plus about 90 min for sound.
